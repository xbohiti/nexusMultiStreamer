# Nexus Multi-Streamer

**One stream from OBS Studio, everywhere at once** — Twitch, YouTube, Kick,
TikTok, and any RTMP/SRT destination. No re-encoding. No monthly subscription.

A Windows app by [RMU Nexus](https://rmu.nexus/programs/multistreamer).

![Nexus Multi-Streamer](https://rmunexus.xyz/images/nexusMultistreamLogo.png)

## Download

Grab the newest installer from the
**[Releases](https://github.com/xbohiti/nexusMultiStreamer/releases/latest)**
page, or use the permanent link (always the latest build):

https://github.com/xbohiti/nexusMultiStreamer/releases/latest/download/NexusMulti-Streamer_Setup.exe

## License key

The installer is free, but the app asks for a license key on first launch.
Buy one at [rmu.nexus](https://rmu.nexus/programs/multistreamer) — the key
arrives by email instantly and activates up to 3 computers.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- OBS Studio
- An internet connection

Everything else — including the streaming engine — is bundled with the
installer.

## Getting started

1. Download and run the installer. (Windows SmartScreen may warn on first
   launch — choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.)
2. Launch Nexus Multi-Streamer and paste your license key when asked.
3. In OBS, press **Start Streaming** once — the app fans your stream out to
   every destination you enable, each with its own title, privacy, and
   schedule.
